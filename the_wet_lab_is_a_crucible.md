> Out of control biology can be fast, but it obeys the laws of physics. A powerful prompt doesn’t put the world at danger overnight.

Ideas for biosecurity
- Checking all DNA synthesis
- Lab access
- Mammalian cell line access

> My point isn’t that doing bad things with biology is possible - it definitely is and we need to be vigilant. My point is that these actions take place in the real world, and offer opportunities for us to detect and respond to these threats in real time. More critically, many of the same approaches we’d need to detect and respond to these threats apply equally to slowing the spread of natural emerging pathogens that threaten us daily.

> Today, biologists are stymied by a vision of our field that doesn’t map to our reality. We struggle daily to get simple things done; biology is hard. Yet we are simultaneously told that we are too powerful to be trusted - wizards that must not be allowed to wield computation.
